    How the book works

    Read the evidence. For each person, enter in pencil who they are, where they were, and whether their word can be trusted. You may hold three entries in pencil at once. When all three are right, they take ink together. When they are not, the book says nothing.

    Every answer can be proved from the paper. Nothing here needs a guess.
