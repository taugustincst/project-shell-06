Privacy Policy — The 9:15 to Blackpool

Effective date: November 3, 2026

The 9:15 to Blackpool ("the Game") is published by AugustInnovations LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles.

Information we collect

None. The Game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (your casebooks, pencil and ink entries, notes, and settings) in your device's local browser storage so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Network access

None. The Game is installed from Google Play with all of its files inside the app, makes no network connections, and plays fully offline. Google Play handles buying the Game; the Game never sees your payment details.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
