# The 9:15 to Blackpool

**A casebook deduction puzzler.** One closed setting per case — a night train, a village fête, a trawler. Manifests, timetables, ticket stubs, interviews. You fill the casebook: who is who, who was where, who is lying. The game confirms only in sets of three correct deductions, so brute force fails and real inference wins. Six cases. One culprit.

Paid on Google Play at $3.99 — a book you play. No ads, no in-app purchases, fully offline.
